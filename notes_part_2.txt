🌐 The Core Domain Architecture (The Squarespace Handshake)
Your domains are registered via Squarespace, but Cloudflare acts as your authoritative DNS manager. When you looked at your clean DNS tables, you saw a vital record left completely intact:
_domainconnect.villagertim.com pointing to _domainconnect.domains.squarespace.com.
Why this matters for your future self:
Do Not Delete This: This specific CNAME record is the umbilical cord between Cloudflare and Squarespace. It ensures that while Cloudflare handles all your advanced web traffic and local home server tunnel routing, Squarespace can still seamlessly validate, secure, and route your underlying domain infrastructure and any tied email authentication protocols without breaking.

Register/Verify: Ensure the domain nameservers in Squarespace are pointed to Cloudflare.
